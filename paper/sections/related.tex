\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

Table~\ref{tab:related} is the comparison we want a reviewer to use.
Prose below only records the one-line difference; it does not repeat the background.

\paragraph{Block-SSD ANNS}
DiskANN, FreshDiskANN, and PipeANN optimize PQ navigation and I/O overlap on block devices~\cite{diskann,freshdiskann,pipeann}.
SPANN and Starling provide alternative hybrid and disk-resident layouts~\cite{spann,starling}.
SPFresh and Filtered-DiskANN extend this family toward updates and filtered search~\cite{spfresh,filtereddiskann}; production vector systems such as Milvus~\cite{milvus} broaden the serving context.
\sys{} is not ``a better \texttt{io\_uring} scheduler.''
Its first-class objects are a CXL-DRAM-shaped window and the committed vector-page fills that must finish before exact reranking.

\paragraph{CXL-DRAM and near-memory ANNS}
CXL-ANNS and Cosmos~\cite{cxlanns,cosmos} --- and second-tier / residual designs~\cite{secondtier,fatrq,hm-ann} --- assume a DRAM- or NVM-priced far tier and optimize for $\sim$100\,ns, not for tens of microseconds of NAND plus a small polluted window.
Recent CXL-aware vector-database restructuring makes the same memory-class tier assumption~\cite{bauhaus}.
Their caches and prefetchers are not transferable at that delay gap (F3).
We do not require Type-2 / PNM compute.

\paragraph{Far-memory runtimes and CXL tiering}
Remote paging systems manage capacity through swapping and isolation~\cite{infiniswap,canvas}, while application-integrated runtimes expose finer-grained control and prefetch~\cite{aifm,leap,rethinkingruntimes}.
CXL tiering systems instead migrate or place hot pages across DRAM-class tiers~\cite{tpp,nomad,neomem,memtierscxl} and study the resulting throughput/capacity tradeoff~\cite{canfarmemory,pond}.
These policies react to generic access locality; \sys{} uses PQ candidate commitment to identify the exact full-precision payload required by reranking.

\paragraph{CXL-SSD hardware and generic software}
DirectCXL and CXL memory pooling establish direct and pooled memory expansion~\cite{directcxl,memorypooling}; CXL-enabled SSD, SkyByte, From-Block-to-Byte, and CMM-H~\cite{cxlssd,skybyte,fromblocktobyte,cmmh} build or evaluate the flash-backed medium.
They do not jointly manage graph-ANNS residency, committed-vector materialization, and stall-aware search.
