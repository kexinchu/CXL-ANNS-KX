\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:concl}

Flash-backed CXL memory offers flash-priced capacity through a memory-semantic window, but is unusable if treated as slow DRAM or mmap'd DiskANN.
\sys{} separates search information from payload movement: wise prefetch materializes the missing pages of a committed rerank set, and continuous batching overlaps each query's materialization with independent work.
Thus exact scoring reads resident bytes while the remaining query-local barrier stays explicit.
