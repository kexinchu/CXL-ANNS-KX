\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\label{sec:discuss}

\paragraph{Proxy versus product.}
The measured CXL-SSD is a software device that fills NAND behind
\texttt{mmap}. Latency cliffs, QD collapse, and promote pollution are
properties of \emph{memory-semantic flash}, not of one driver. Absolute
QPS and lock artifacts may move on a hardware BAR path; we will not
relabel the proxy as a shipping CXL.mem SSD.

\paragraph{One-copy is a deployment claim, not a result.}
Figure~\ref{fig:eval-scale}b is a provisioning model. Concurrent
multi-host mapping needs CXL 3.x Shared FAM, a fabric manager, and a
read-only publish protocol~\cite{cxl30}. CXL 2.0 pooling is not that.
Even with one copy, device-side IOPS remain shared: one-copy removes
capacity replication, not bandwidth replication.

\paragraph{DiskANN remains the block-path champion.}
PQ navigation plus deep \texttt{aio} is extremely good at not reading
full-precision vectors. \sys{} is the runtime for the tier where those
vectors \emph{do} live in a load/store flash address space. A residual
QPS gap on the PQ-on block path is an expected con, not a surprise we
omit.

\paragraph{Layout tax is dimension-dependent.}
Record-vs-page (F2) is strongest when a vector is comparable to a page.
Low-dimension corpora need the page-bin experiment; we will not
universalize a 1024-d result.

\paragraph{What would change our mind.}
If a hardware CXL-SSD with device-side copy saturates a wide internal
bus, the \emph{async issue} path stays, the \emph{depth} target changes.
If Shared FAM is available, D3 becomes a multi-host coherence/publish
problem we have not solved. If bounded promote never beats demand on
the primary corpus, contribution~3 shrinks to shared hot set $+$
stall-aware scheduling and we will say so.
